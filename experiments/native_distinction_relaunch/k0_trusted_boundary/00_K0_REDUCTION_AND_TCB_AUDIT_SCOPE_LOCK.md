## Claim under attack

Candidate C: Given an EXTERNALLY specified decision contract, native-system descriptions, and an explicit trust/authorization root, EEQ can automatically construct, independently check, and maintain the **minimum sufficient evidence applicability boundary** across heterogeneous systems without comparable domain-specific semantic engineering. This is a *hypothesis*, not an established method or novelty result.

Try to refute C with:
1. Direct code-level provenance of where E2/P2's trusted assumptions REALLY live; include trusted witness generators, native canonicalizer, authority/roster/time assumptions and detached vs orchestrated verifier.
2. Faithful bounded reduction to query completeness/certain answers, goal-directed credential discovery/delegation logic, certifying compilation/proof-carrying code, provenance/IVM, dynamic epistemic models and distributed consistent cuts.
3. Same-source/same-permission strong B9 which may copy **all** EEQ certificates, extraction, proof checkers, native libraries, action planning and maintenance.
4. Two complementary kill cases:
   - External completeness/authority/cut witnesses already available: existing compositional methods can construct a scoped warrant (so the claimed capability is not unique).
   - The required completeness/authority/temporal witness does not exist or is not legally observable: no sound algorithm can conclusively decide between indistinguishable opposite-effect worlds (so EEQ is not an exception).
5. A code-level DETACHED witness self-consistency attack: if the standalone P2 checker sees only `program` and `external`, verify from code whether a forged pair with identical source-digest *references* passes even though the genuine sources remain unchanged. This is a **static logical audit**, NOT a replay of P1 nor a new proof the full CI workflow is compromised.

- `OBSERVATIONAL_NO_GO_WITHOUT_PREMISES`: missing qualified observation/closure yields indistinguishable histories; sound global determination impossible.

- `NONREDUCIBLE_THEORETICAL_RESIDUAL_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`: absent a precise property that fails preservation in a full-strength reduction, NO novel theorem.
