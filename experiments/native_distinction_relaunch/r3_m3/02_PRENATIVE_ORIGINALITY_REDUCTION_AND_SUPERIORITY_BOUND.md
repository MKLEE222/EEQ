## Foundational logical ceiling (do not hide this)

Define B9 FULL as a fully informed hand-engineered sufficient state
that may consume ALL lawful, same-freshness native policies, source
qualification inputs, registered actions and decision contracts.
If "B9" is instead defined as the unrestricted class of ALL computable
methods given identical sources/budgets, it contains an implementation
that copies EEQ; no strict expressive or accuracy dominance of EEQ over
that unrestricted class can be a meaningful noncircular theorem.

For any fixed complete native instance and exact task where such a B9
already gets every action decision right, accuracy cannot be exceeded
on that exact finite set. Refuse to redefine B9 into a version-only,
unknown-as-absent or no-dependency-tracking straw baseline.

A valid method-contribution study MUST either identify a genuinely
different scientifically necessary task/object (e.g. constructing and
sustaining evidence-qualified decision state from independent original
source authorities), or preregister a FAIR realistic resource/engineering
task with strong, implemented, reusable B9 and closest generic prior
art (provenance, three-valued logic and incremental maintenance).

## Claim-relative visibility and non-identifiability

Let O_t be the actual original source evidence observed by an agent,
Q_t the qualification/authenticity/freshness information available
under a declared source/actor/claim contract, and W(O_t,Q_t) the family
of lawful native completions that agree on those observed sources.
A certificate for a decision D of claim c is SOUND only if every
admissible world consistent with its evidence and scoped contract
supports that D. In particular:

 Certify ACCEPT => for every w in W(O_t,Q_t), D_w(c)=ACCEPT.
 Certify REJECT => for every w in W(O_t,Q_t), D_w(c)=REJECT.

If there are w0,w1 that agree on all observed known-source bytes,
yet D_w0(c)=ACCEPT and D_w1(c)=REJECT due to an additional qualified
source not covered by O_t, then no decoder of those observed bytes can
certify either unconditional result.

This is classical possible-world CERTAIN ANSWERS / epistemic
indistinguishability, NOT novel EEQ mathematics. It is important because
it identifies an actual unsatisfied G6/C1 source-inventory obligation.

Native R3-M3 will pressure precisely such a two-world distinction with
two unchanged old VAP Bindings and a newly added matching third Binding.
Its source-only predictions were frozen ahead of native scoring; it is
not an independent transfer family.

## Why a cryptographic/content hash is insufficient

A content digest proves which bytes were seen; it does NOT prove the
membership of all currently relevant policy sources, nor the identity
and access rights of the source collector, nor that an earlier list
remained current after an admission-source mutation.

The minimal missing components for an auditable claim might include:
- qualified source authority and actor/claim scope;
- independently established enumeration rights for all relevant
  source classes (may require privileged native actors);
- original complete LIST per source class at a specific time/version;
- a gap-free, monitored LIST->WATCH relationship for later epochs or
  a fresh re-fetch and explicit re-attestation;
- independent semantics for how binding/policy/params/labels/admission
  webhooks change relevance;
- action-relative source effect, with invalidation on uncertainty;
- full original policy bytes plus native oracle record kept for audit.

A LIST snapshot alone is NOT a universally transferable completeness
proof or a guarantee of no changes between two snapshots.
Kubernetes resourceVersions are OPAQUE and should not be compared
numerically. Cross-resource LIST snapshots are not one atomic snapshot.
Admission webhooks and other controllers are OUTSIDE the current
controlled VAP source scope unless separately authorized and accounted.

## Asymmetric support, and why this is not already originality

Under additive Deny with fresh qualified evidence, one sufficient
applicable Deny proof stays sufficient if unrelated new Deny-only
sources are added; to prove ACCEPT requires all potentially denying
effective sources either ruled out or proven to pass.
But this is ordinary monotone witness / possible-world inference, and
OASIS XACML 3.0 already has Deny-overrides and effect-specific
Indeterminate(D,P,DP). A strong B9 can implement the same witness logic.

Similarly, source dependency read-set cache invalidation, provenance
semiring expressions and self-adjusting incremental computation are
known fields. Merely packaging old-root TUF signatures and Kubernetes
binding selectors into one JSON schema does NOT establish a general
new algorithm. Existing relevant sources:
  https://docs.oasis-open.org/xacml/3.0/xacml-3.0-core-spec-cos01-en.html
  https://doi.org/10.1145/1265530.1265535
