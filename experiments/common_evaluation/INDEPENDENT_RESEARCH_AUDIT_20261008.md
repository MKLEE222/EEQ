## Evaluation rubric

- A green workflow proves software execution under its assertions only.
- `accuracy=1.0` for injective representations does not prove nontrivial
  decision sufficiency or compression.
- A partial sufficient-blocker predictor does not certify an entire native
  governance family.
- A synthetic finite-state theorem does not certify that V0/C1-C3 can be
  extracted from unbounded real native evidence.
- Full method innovation remains a **research hypothesis**, not yet a
  manuscript claim.
